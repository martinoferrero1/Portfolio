# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript; deployment target: Vercel.

## Users

Recruiters and potential clients evaluating Martino Ferrero's professional experience and qualifications.

## Product Purpose

A professional portfolio for both audiences. It should help visitors assess Martino's qualifications and decide whether to contact him; the preferred visitor action is still undecided.

## Positioning

Martino works across backend engineering, generative AI, computer vision, and medical imaging research. Keep claims grounded in the available evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The project currently has no application scaffold or implemented product capabilities.
- The supplied CV describes systems engineering, backend engineering, AI and machine learning work, and research in medical imaging.
- The portfolio will be in English, with Home, About, Projects, Skills, and Contact on one continuously scrolling page.
- Martino is pursuing a doctorate at INTIA focused on AI for medical image processing and related research projects.
- Additional project material includes Lookeate's repository, Neurollow's public social accounts, and Martino's description of a team project for quarry process digitalization and automation.
- Martino supplied a photo and caricature for use in the portfolio.

## Evidence on Hand

- `Martino Ferrero - CV.pdf`: professional summary, experience, projects, education, skills, and contact links.
- Lookeate: https://github.com/martinoferrero1/multimodal-clothing-assistance-system
- Neurollow: https://www.linkedin.com/company/neurollow/ and https://www.instagram.com/neurollow/
- `Foto.jfif` and `Caricatura.png`: supplied personal imagery.

## Product Principles

- Keep professional claims grounded in the supplied CV and the project sources Martino provided.
- Serve recruiters and potential clients as distinct audiences with different evaluation needs.
- Make the next step for visitors clear once Martino chooses the preferred action.
- Present work in AI and software engineering in concrete terms.

## Brand Commitments

- The portfolio should feel formal and restrained: mostly black, with subtle dark blue and violet accents, mostly white text, and conventional readable typography.
- Favor a centered composition, a small understated personal image, simple buttons and icons, and a continuous page without modal overlays or excessive cards.
- Visual interest should come mainly from considered scroll reveals, scrollytelling, parallax, and staggered motion. Neural network references should be sparse and unobtrusive.
